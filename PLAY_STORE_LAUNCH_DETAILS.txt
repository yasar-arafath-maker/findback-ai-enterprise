• Full Description:
  FindBack AI Enterprise is an intelligent, multi-modal lost and found item recovery platform designed for campuses, organizations, public spaces, and communities. Combining 64-bit text SimHash fingerprinting, Gemini AI image similarity analysis, Haversine GPS geospatial proximity indexing, and temporal decay algorithms, FindBack AI connects item finders with rightful owners automatically.

  KEY FEATURES & CAPABILITIES:

  🔍 Multi-Modal AI Match Engine
  • Gemini AI Vision: Automatically extracts feature tags and visual similarity matrices from item photos.
  • SimHash Text Fingerprinting: Generates 64-bit cryptographic text signatures of item descriptions to detect duplicate reports instantly.
  • Spatial & Temporal Proximity: Uses Haversine GPS distance calculation and time-elapsed decay to rank high-confidence matches.

  🔒 Privacy-Preserving Protection & Relays
  • Smart Anti-Loss QR Tags: Attach printable QR tags to your laptops, keys, and bags. Finders scan with any camera to contact you safely.
  • Masked Contact Relays: Virtual masked phone trunks and encrypted chat keep your phone number and personal email strictly private.
  • Zero-Knowledge Evidence Isolation: Claim evidence (purchase invoices, lockscreen wallpapers) is guarded via Row-Level Security (RLS) and is visible ONLY to verifying administrators.

  📜 Cryptographic Verification & Finality
  • 6-Digit Verification Handovers: Handover requires one-time verification codes generated at the time of claim finalization.
  • SHA-256 Recovery Certificates: Issues cryptographically signed PDF handover certificates for audit compliance and fraud prevention.

  HOW IT WORKS:
  1. Report: Submit a lost or found item report with photo, location, and key details.
  2. AI Match: Our AI engine continuously scans and calculates similarity scores across active reports.
  3. Claim & Verify: Verify ownership via secure zero-knowledge evidence submission.
  4. Handover: Complete the item recovery using a 6-digit OTP code and receive a SHA-256 digital certificate.
