8. Full Description
FindBack AI is an intelligent, multi-modal lost and found item recovery platform designed for campuses, enterprises, public transit systems, and communities. Combining Google Gemini AI vision analysis, 64-bit text SimHash fingerprinting, Haversine GPS geospatial proximity indexing, and temporal decay scoring algorithms, FindBack AI connects item finders with rightful owners automatically, quickly, and securely.

CORE CAPABILITIES & HIGHLIGHTS

1. Multi-Modal AI Match Engine
• Gemini AI Vision Processing: Automatically analyzes item photographs to extract visual attributes, color signatures, and feature vector matrices.
• SimHash Text Fingerprinting: Generates 64-bit cryptographic text signatures of item descriptions to detect duplicate reports and match phrasing.
• Spatial & Temporal Proximity: Computes real-time GPS distance using the Haversine formula combined with time-decay scoring to prioritize high-probability matches.

2. Privacy-First Protection & Relays
• Smart Anti-Loss QR Tags: Generate and print custom QR code tags for laptops, bags, wallets, keys, and personal belongings. Anyone who finds your item can scan the tag to initiate recovery.
• Privacy-Masked Communication: Finders and owners communicate through encrypted, anonymized relay channels that protect personal telephone numbers and email addresses.
• Zero-Knowledge Evidence Isolation: Claim evidence such as purchase receipts and serial numbers is guarded with Row-Level Security (RLS) and is visible only to authorized verifying administrators.

3. Cryptographic Verification & Handover
• 6-Digit Verification Handover: Physical return requires one-time cryptographically generated verification codes to eliminate false claims.
• SHA-256 Digital Recovery Certificates: Automatically issues tamper-proof PDF handover certificates containing SHA-256 cryptographic hashes for enterprise audit compliance.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Report: Submit a lost or found report with item description, category, photo, and approximate location.
2. AI Match: The multi-modal matching engine continuously compares active records and generates confidence scores.
3. Claim & Verify: Claimants submit zero-knowledge evidence verified by platform administrators.
4. Secure Handover: Meet at designated security points, exchange the 6-digit OTP, and receive your digital certificate.
